# ADR-NNNN — Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed (draft — not ratified)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Relates to:** principles, documents, or ADRs this touches

## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints, pressures, or risks are
in play? Written so a reader who wasn't there understands the problem before
seeing the answer. State facts, not the conclusion.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the present tense. Include the parts
that are binding — the specific guarantees, the things that are non-negotiable
— rather than a general direction.

## Consequences

What follows from this, good and bad. Include the costs we are knowingly
accepting; an ADR with only benefits is not being honest about the trade-off.
Note anything this makes harder, any obligation it creates, and any test or
follow-up work it requires.

## Alternatives considered

Each option we genuinely weighed, what it would have bought us, and why it
lost. "Deferred, not rejected" is a valid outcome and should say what would
bring it back. This section is the most valuable part of the document a year
from now.
